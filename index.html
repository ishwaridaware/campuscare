How Campus Care Works

Replacing physical lost-and-found notice boards with a transparent 3-step digital flow.

01

1. Post a Quick Report

Lost your keys or found someone's ID card? Submit the details, campus location, and photo in under a minute.

02

2. Search & Match

Search by category, date, or specific building. Filter between lost items and found items awaiting their owners.

03

3. Safely Reclaim

Reach out to the reporter or visit the designated campus security station to verify ownership and collect your item.
